# 21 — Lessons Matrix

Every row is a lesson extracted from the five review corpora. "Our rule" is the design decision
that captures the upside and removes the downside.

## 1. Per-game scorecard

### A Game About Digging a Hole `[AGADH]` — 89% positive, 20,098 reviews, $5, 1–2 h

| Keep | Why it works | Avoid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zen, addictive dig→sell→upgrade loop | #1 praise theme; "hypnotic", "meditative" | 1–3 h runtime for $5 | "Paid demo"; refund-window jokes |
| Shovel→drill power escalation | "switches from diggy digging to straight up Hoovering" | Forced stealth-horror mole finale that disables all tools | "Ruined by its endgame"; genre bait-and-switch |
| Honest title, no MTX, no fluff | "0% scam, 0% P2W" | Upgrades maxed at ~15% depth | Money pointless before the best ores |
| Cheap coffee-price fun | "cost of a cup of coffee" | Achievements locked behind NG+ | "Incredibly scummy"; buggy counters |
| Voxel charm, satisfying dig audio, Deck support | "that satisfying shovel crunch" | Floating 1-voxel dirt, fall damage from specks | Snags movement, kills speedruns |
| Meme/humor goodwill, solo-dev story | "Diggy diggy hole" | Click-per-cut digging, no keybinds/graphics/FOV/cloud | RSI warnings; "no key binding settings" |
| The twist has genuine fans | "M. Night Shyamalan" comparisons | Crashes on selling/buying; hole not saved | "Fatal Error on selling ore"; lost hours |

**Our version:** keep the loop, the escalation, the honesty, the sound; fix runtime (10–14 h),
economy (max at 85% depth + eternal sinks), finale (tools stay, real reward), options (full settings
suite), dirt (flood-fill cleanup), and saves (terrain persisted).

### Keep Digging `[KD]` — 80% positive, 3,266 reviews, $7, 0.5–7 h

| Keep | Why it works | Avoid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Co-op chaos with friends | "primal satisfaction… racing a friend" | 5–15 FPS on high-end GPUs, shader compile walls, crashes | #1 complaint; BSoD/crypto suspicions |
| "One more dig" addiction | "one more dig turned into 3 hours" | Straight-down trivializes everything | Devs admitted it in patch notes |
| Cheap price / one-night party value | "each got our $5 worth" | Host-only bones/buttons/achievements; money not shared | Multiplayer ownership problems |
| Humor, chimp skins, emotes | "worshiping the chimpanzee" | Clunky UI, no keybinds, no hold-to-dig, no sprint | Carpal tunnel complaints |
| NG+ keeps gear, new skins | "upgrades retained" | Asset-flip/rip-off accusations | "Blatant ripoff of A Game About Digging a Hole" |
| Responsive devs, free 2.0 | "many another publisher would have sold as DLC" | 2.0 "patched in stress", RNG blueprints, killed the dumb fun | Community split; legacy players alienated |
| Japanese/Chinese audience love for atmosphere | "civilization evolves as you go deeper" | Dynamite trap upgrade (host-only scaling) | "bombs do nothing" |

**Our version:** co-op later with parity (`16`), anti-straight-down via lateral gates (`03`),
performance as a P0 (`15`), co-op shared money/unlocks, honest original art (`17`),
legacy/optional balance branches (`20`).

### Meltopia `[MELT]` — 86% positive, 1,722 reviews, $7, 5–8 h

| Keep | Why it works | Avoid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deeply relaxing melt loop | #1 praise; "meditative", "second-screen" | Tesla gun invalidates flamethrower investment | #1 negative; "progression grinds to a halt" |
| Visible tool upgrades (bolted-on modules) | "each upgrade really makes a difference" | No map; identical tunnels | "Would've gone insane without the hand-drawn guide map" |
| Exploration, secrets, big maps | "It's like a metroidvania version" | Late economy collapses; 100k with nothing to buy | Hours of walking for no purpose |
| No enemies, freeze-not-death | "you don't die, you freeze instead" | Shovel is slow, unupgradable, click-spam | "I hate it. I hate it so much." |
| Atmosphere, glowing ice, sparkles | "truly beautiful" | Autosave freezes 5–10 s; lost saves; no cloud; one slot | "It's 2026, not 1990" |
| Easy 100% in one run | "able to 100% in 5 hours" | 45/46 meta-achievement bug | Completionists stuck forever |
| Near-daily patches, free content | "devs are incredibly active" | August heat rebalance made cozy players freeze | "Patched in stress" analog; sentiment dropped |

**Our version:** parallel tools that never invalidate (`04`), first-class map (`12`),
economy with eternal sinks (`06`), no dud tool, freeze-free async saves + cloud (`15`), balance
changes opt-in/beta-first (`20`), one-run 100% (`18`).

### One Man's Trash `[OMT]` — 86% positive, 330 reviews, $5–7, 2.5–6 h

| Keep | Why it works | Avoid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vacuum + dirt-spitting (no leftover specks, build stairs) | Direct improvement noted by players | Unadvertised worms/horror | #1 complaint across languages |
| Hypnotic "zone out" loop | "No thoughts, head empty, I dig hole" | Barely longer than the demo | "Just play the Demo" |
| Collectibles, references, weird deep finds | "deeper you go, weirder it gets" | Invisible +5% upgrades, tier/license gating | "Nothing changes in the game" |
| Placed lighting, changing environments | "garlands offering a multitude of colors" | Rope-only traversal, health RNG sandwiches | "AFK for 5 minutes to heal" |
| Fair price, cozy mode added later | "cheaper than a beer" | No autosave; crashes; broken achievements | Lost hours |
| Dev responsiveness / new modes | "new game modes… cozy mode without worms" | Slot machine with 75.7% return | "Do not bet, kids" |

**Our version:** vacuum/terraform as core tools (`04` `05`), no leftover voxels by construction,
horror strictly opt-in (`08`), 10–14 h at $10 (`19`), every upgrade visible (`06`), four ways up
(`07`), cosmetics are freely chosen, slot machine decorative with cosmetic-only outcomes.

### The Spotter: Dig or Die `[SPOT]` — 85% positive, 1,859 reviews, $13, 6–12 h

| Keep | Why it works | Avoid | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Addictive day/night greedy-dig loop | "played 2 hours and didn't know it" | Stamina/beer + constant surface trips | #1 complaint: "will you let me dig or not?!" |
| Tangible early progression | "you really feel like leveling up makes a difference" | Late tiers = padding; mandatory expensive tier before finale | "40 to 60 more trips to the bottom" |
| Handcrafted underground secrets | "single genuine achievement is its underground world" | Fixed turret branch (no respec) | "I HATE THAT IN ORDER TO TRY NEW TURRET I MUST START OVER" |
| Manual night defense FPS layer | "you are actively involved in the fights" | Vague quests, no map, fixed keys, no cloud | "Players often feel lost" |
| Humor, dog, charm | "let your dog get abducted" | Unskippable parkour/instadeath/horror rooms | "This game is a horror game in disguise" |
| Free updates, fast patches, no EA | "not charging for DLC" | 1.1 update invalidated saves/guides | "Having to start from zero is frustrating" |
| $10–13 accepted for 10–30 h of content | "for the price of a Starbucks drink" | Ending retcon ("it was a game show") + no NG+/endgame | "All that time digging? Pointless" |
| | | Motion sickness/eye strain, drill speed tied to FPS | Unusually frequent for non-VR |

**Our version:** soft pressure that never steals loot (`07`), optional day/night-style danger
(`08`), free respec (`06`), map + quest clarity (`12`), non-invalidating updates (`20`),
payoff ending + full post-game (`09` `10`), frame-rate independence enforced (`15`),
mandatory improvements happen in dark rooms only (`14`).

## 2. Aggregated praise themes → design commitments

| Praise theme | Corpora where top-ranked | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Satisfying / addictive / zen loop | all five | Keep loop sacred; no friction creep (`02` `07`) |
| Relaxing / cozy / podcast-friendly | all five | Cozy default, music optional, pause anywhere (`01` `13`) |
| Progression you can feel | `[MELT]` `[SPOT]` `[AGADH]` | Visible upgrades, audible deltas (`06`) |
| Discoveries, secrets, weird depths | `[OMT]` `[SPOT]` `[MELT]` | Handcrafted POI density (`03`) |
| Humor and personality | `[AGADH]` `[KD]` `[SPOT]` | Text-first humor, toggleable (`10` `13`) |
| Fair price | all five | $9.99 with 10+ h and post-game (`19`) |
| Responsive devs, free updates | `[KD]` `[MELT]` `[SPOT]` | Patch discipline + roadmap (`20`) |
| Presentation, atmosphere, audio | `[AGADH]` `[MELT]` `[OMT]` `[SPOT]` | Cohesive hand-made art + layered audio (`17`) |
| Co-op chaos | `[KD]` | Co-op later, with parity (`16`) |
| Vacuum/terraform QoL | `[OMT]` | Core tools (`04`) |
| Easy fair 100% | `[MELT]` `[KD]` | One-run deterministic achievements (`18`) |

## 3. Aggregated criticism themes → forbidden patterns

| Criticism theme | Corpora | Forbidden pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Too short / paid demo | `[AGADH]` `[KD]` `[OMT]` `[MELT]` | Critical path < 8 h at > $7 |
| Forced genre shift / tools disabled | `[AGADH]` `[SPOT]` | Any story sequence that removes agency or equipment |
| Unadvertised horror | `[OMT]` `[AGADH]` `[SPOT]` | Horror without explicit consent; horror on critical path |
| Economy dead-ends | `[AGADH]` `[MELT]` `[OMT]` | Maxed purchases before 60% depth; money with no sink |
| Invisible upgrades | `[OMT]` `[KD]` `[MELT]` (Tesla) | Upgrades without visible/audible change; invalidating new tools |
| No map / lost | `[MELT]` `[SPOT]` | Shipping without a full map system |
| Performance / crashes | `[KD]` `[MELT]` `[AGADH]` `[OMT]` `[SPOT]` | Single-digit FPS, shader walls, crash-on-core-action, FPS decay |
| Save loss / no cloud / freezes | all four PC games | Non-atomic saves, save freezes, no cloud, one slot |
| RSI / click-spam | `[AGADH]` `[OMT]` `[MELT]` | Any required repeated clicking; no hold-to-dig |
| Motion sickness / no options | all five | FOV/blur/shake/bob settings missing; motion-sick defaults |
| Fixed/greyed-out design choices | `[SPOT]` `[KD]` | Irreversible builds; RNG replacing shops |
| Grind walls / padding | `[SPOT]` `[MELT]` `[KD]` | Mandatory repeat trips to progress the critical path |
| Ending betrayal | `[AGADH]` `[SPOT]` `[MELT]` | Retcon/empty reward/punishing finale |
| Asset flip / AI slop | `[KD]` `[MELT]` | Unedited marketplace assets; AI-generated content |
| Hostile/broken achievements | `[AGADH]` `[MELT]` `[SPOT]` `[KD]` | NG+-locked, RNG, bugged, host-only, false "no cheating" |
| No updates / broken updates | `[AGADH]` `[KD]` `[MELT]` `[SPOT]` | Abandonment; patches that invalidate saves or cozy balance |

## 4. Numeric anchors

| Metric | Worst offender | Best in set | Our target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median playtime | `[AGADH]` 3.4 h | `[SPOT]` 7.6 h | 12–18 h median |
| Critical path | `[AGADH]` 1–2 h | `[SPOT]` 6–12 h | 10–14 h |
| Price / hour | `[AGADH]` ~$2.5/h | `[SPOT]` ~$1.2/h | ≤ $1/h |
| Performance complaints | `[KD]` #1 theme | `[AGADH]` moderate | < 3% of reviews |
| Save-related complaints | `[MELT]` `[AGADH]` `[SPOT]` | — | 0 tolerance |
| Content-completion 100% | `[AGADH]` NG+-locked | `[MELT]` `[KD]` one run | One run, deterministic |
