Companion to `02A_L_REGISTER.md`. All delegated proposals are now confirmed by the user.

**Statuses:** `PENDING` · `OPEN` · `DISCUSSING` · `CONFIRMED` · `REVISED` · `REJECTED` · `DELEGATED` · `DEFERRED`

**Rules**

1. I never mark anything `CONFIRMED` on your behalf.
2. Bulk answers are allowed at group level, but every row still gets its own status line.
3. Changing a decision requires a new dated entry in the log; old entries are struck through, never deleted.
4. A decision later contradicted by review evidence is flagged, not silently overridden.
5. **No business, marketing, production, resourcing, team, budget, timeline or implementation
   questions.** Those are the developer's call. Questions stay on game design.
6. Questions must be about the game itself; minimize references to older documents.
7. **No silent assumptions:** even ideas that look settled are open to re-questioning. Ask.
8. **No exact-number questions:** values are prototype-tuned; questions stay at rules/variations level.
